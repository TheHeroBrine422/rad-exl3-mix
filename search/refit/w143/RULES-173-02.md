## Rows (first wave: 67 A8 mixes, `mkspecs143.py`)
Every container: hostd's `.mix` (trunk level packs + head / embedding donors on the V2-3.00 base) plus
`act <w> a8g128` on all 336 trunk weights (w132's hostd patch, `"act": "a8g128"`). Each one is scored once on
**fit129** (w129's calib fit-pool slice, 127,793 scored positions). No new documents, never verify, never public.
- **A1** (7): uniform K0.5, 0.625, 0.75, 0.875, 1, 1.125, 1.25 with head u1.5 and embedding e2.
- **A2** (12): base = A1's uniform K1; each of the 6 groups × 2 depth halves (L0–31 / L32–63) → K0.625.
- **C1** (20): w129's 20 uniform rows (K1.25–K7, V2 head and embedding) in A8.
- **C2** (24): w129's group swaps on the donor K−1 side (base 2.5 → 1.5 and base 4 → 3, 6 groups × 2 halves) in A8.
- **B** (4): uniform K7.5; uniform K7; K7 with all ffn_gu → K7.5; K7 with all ffn_down → K7.5 (head u7,
  embedding e5). The uniform K7 row is one more than the plan: it anchors the two swaps on the same head / embedding.

Container check: `store/census.py MIX.rad --mix MIX.mix --base <the V2-3.00 container>` on every container before it is deleted. A
mismatch drops the row and is reported (w142's census is not built yet; store/census.py does the same check).

## Fit rows
Obs = w133's final-fit rows (ds-P search rows + w129's 90 fit129 rows + w135's cal174 rows) + w135's 18 a8-174 rows
(mode a8) + the 67 new rows. New rows: label `fit129` (the same documents as w129's rows, so one label offset),
`mode: a8`, SE = the per-document delta-method SE of log(KLD / KLD of the A8 uniform K2.5 row) on fit129 (fit129.py's
`lse`). A row of the plan that failed scoring twice or failed the census is left out and listed.
Deviation from calplan173 (label `fit129-a8`): with its own label, the A8 rows' level would be absorbed by a free
offset, and C1 (the same mixes as w129's f16 rows, on the same documents) could not identify the A8 term.

## Models (fitted on colo with m133 / fin133; candidates173's M1 + ext "mode")
- **F1** = M1 + mode (per-mode P (1 + a) + c, as in m133).
- **F2** = F1 + kfit3's saturation `sat` (P → C (1 − e^(−P / C))).
- **F3** = F1 with the part curvature (lam2) linearised outside the logP range of the fit's rows (continuous value and
  slope at the edges), so extrapolation stays monotone. No new parameters.

**Selection** (in this order):
1. Hard requirement: the model's uniform-level curve (base head / embedding, `fin133.py curve`) is strictly
   decreasing in K over every level offered to the solve (K ≥ 0.5; K0.125–0.375 have no rows and are not offered).
2. Among those that pass: the lowest 5-fold CV weighted MSE (weights 1 / (SE² + 0.02²), log scale, label offset
   profiled on the training rows) on the 19 sub-1 rows (A1 + A2; folds = rows sorted by tag, index mod 5, each fold's
   rows held out of the fit).
3. If the next simpler model (fewer parameters: F1 = F3 < F2, F1 before F3) is within 10 % of the best CV MSE, take the
   simpler one.
If none passes 1, report it and solve with F-best at `--min-k 1.25` (sizes 1.0 / 1.25 then stay unsolved).

Also reported (no decision): the fitted A8 terms (a, c) and the per-level C1 / w129 log ratio A8 − f16 with paired CIs
(per-document bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 1730); the sub-1 rows' residuals under w133's fit-M1-full (the
"before") and under the chosen model; C2 group-swap ratios A8 vs f16 (paired per pair).
