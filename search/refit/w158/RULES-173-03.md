**V2-prior ablation** (lead note 11-13 12:45 PM): **M1m-noV2** = M1m with every V2-era prior sd × 4 (theta smoothness 0.5 → 2,
theta shrinkage 4 → 16, head vs trunk 6 → 24, kappa second difference 1 → 4, family-group deviation 0.3 → 1.2, family factor
1 → 4); no V2 row exists in the data. Compared on (a) 5-fold CV weighted MSE of log KLD over all wave-1 + 2 rows (weights
1 / (SE² + 0.02²), folds = rows sorted by tag, index mod 5, label offset profiled on the training rows), (b) rank accuracy: sign
agreement of predicted vs measured log ratio vs base on the held-out G / H / E / B / S2 rows whose |measured| > 2 SE, (c)
"T by time": fit on wave 1, predict wave 2; "P / S": the A3 rows and the interaction test's single moves (fresh slice:
predicted vs measured log ratio vs its base) — reported, not deciding. (d) pick movement: bytes moved / tensors changed between
the two fits' picks per size. **Adopt M1m-noV2 iff its CV wMSE ≤ 1.10 × M1m's** (not worse; simpler priors, current data).
**Sub-1 pricing** (w143's rule, unchanged): F1 = the adopted fit; F2 = F1 + kfit3's saturation `sat`; F3 = F1 with lam2
linearised outside the logP range of the rows (continuous value and slope). (1) Hard requirement: the uniform-level curve (head
u6.5, emb t8m) strictly decreasing in K over every offered level (K ≥ 0.5). (2) Among those passing: lowest 5-fold CV wMSE on
the 17 sub-1 rows (folds by tag, index mod 5, each fold held out of the fit). (3) A simpler model (F1 = F3 < F2; F1 before F3)
within 10 % of the best CV wins. None passes (1) → report, solve with `--min-k 1` (sizes 1.0 / 1.25 unsolved).
Until wave 2 lands, the picks use K ≥ 1 and the sub-1 sizes are marked pending.
