## Rollback

1. Stop the driver. Restore the backed-up `driver.json` and `registry.json` from `run/pre188/` (or remove the `s186*` keys and
   restore `cap`, `pool`, `cross_share0`, `reseed`, as in `SWITCH-strata.md` step 10).
2. `git apply -R -p1 search/v3/pool188/driver-pool.patch` (reverts driver.py, moves.py; removes poolrule.py). If you committed
   the change, `git revert` the commit instead.
3. Restart the driver. The old code ignores the `pool`, `reseed_*` and `reselect` events and keeps the incumbents from the
   `incumbent` events; the state is consistent with the old driver (incumbents only).
4. Hosts: `SWITCH-strata.md` step 10 (restore the copied files, restart the daemon).

## What to watch for in the first hours (not gates)

- Cost: a full pool of 3 is rescored at every rotation (about 3 full evals per size, every size in the run); the re-seed
  adds up to 3 candidates per listed size once (about 24 full evals at the switch). Re-seed and rescore evals are evals,
  not decisions: they do not count towards `rotate_after`.
- A `reselect` event at each rotation lists the members' statistics against the new incumbent. A switch of incumbent at
  rotation is the pool doing its job; check it is not a single document (RULES-picks check: a document > 20 % of a part's KL).
- `status.txt`: the `pool` field shows the member count per size; a size stuck at 1 after the re-seed means its joins never
  beat the worst member (normal early on).
