## Background (code read, not data)

`refit_scales` (fork `quantize.py` ~1730–1800, called with `H_orig` only) alternates, 2 rounds, a per-output-column
`svh` step (closed form; separable, so a diagonal H_out cannot change it) and a per-input-row `suh` step that solves
`((Q Qᵀ) ∘ H) r = rowsum(Q ∘ H W)` with every output column weighted alike, in the **damped** H. LDLQ before it
minimises the two-sided objective. rxq's `quant.refit = true` is stamped but not wired (always on).

**Score.** w81's f2 (`local/workers/w81/f2calc.py` arithmetic, fp64 on the CPU, copied as `w98/f2calc98.py` with only
paths changed): f2 = Σ_n h_n e_n / Σ_n h_n r_n, e_n = (W − Wq)_n H (W − Wq)_nᵀ, r_n = W_n H W_nᵀ, H = the raw calib H_in
of the tensor's group, h = the raw bn1920 diagonal. f1 = the same with h = 1 (reported, no condition).
ρ(arm) = f2(arm) / f2(cur) per tensor × K (13 tensors × 3 K).

**Checks before any arm is read** (a failure stops the read until explained):
- c1 (no-op): the stored t-files of L10 / L39 at K2.5 bit-identical (tensor data) to w92's fs0 jobs of the same layers,
  when fs0 exists with the same settings stamp (it is the same spec and seed).
- c2: the in-job f2 of `cur` (fp32 on the card) within 2e-3 relative of f2calc98's (fp64, CPU) on all 39 cells.
- c3 (#131's check on real jobs): per tensor, Σ_n e_n / Σ_n r_n of the diag file equals the logged `proxy_err` to
  1e-6 relative.

**Pass rule (per arm X ∈ {none, hout, undamped}).** Targets T = {L10 in_proj_qkv, L10 in_proj_z, L39 q_proj} × {K0.5,
K2.5, K4} (9 cells).
- (a) geo-mean of ρ(X) over T ≤ 0.990, **and**
- (b) no family worse: for each of the 13 tensors the geo-mean of ρ(X) over the 3 K ≤ 1.001, and no single cell > 1.005.

If several arms pass, the candidate is the one with the lowest geo over T. If `hout` and `undamped` both pass,
`hout+undamped` is the candidate instead when it also passes (a) and (b) and its geo over T is lower than both.
If `none` is the candidate: "polish harmful under H_out". No arm passes → #130 negative, `refit = true` stays, no 27B
pair. A best arm with T-geo in (0.997, 1.000] is labelled "drop (< 0.3%)".

## #131 · Log-once instrumentation (code, ~0 runtime)

Every rxq quant job also writes, per tensor, `diag-<key>.safetensors` (written before its t-file; `rxq assemble`
ignores it) and a `diag` entry in its log.jsonl record:
- per output channel n (fp32): e1 = (EᵀHE)_nn of the stored weight (E = W − Wq, H = the undamped calib H_in, the
  refit's error metric), e2 = h_n · e1_n (H_out levels), e1_pre / e2_pre (before the refit), x1 = q_nᵀ H e_n and
  d1 = q_nᵀ H q_n (the error after any later per-channel rescale c: e1 − 2(c−1)·x1 + (c−1)²·d1: C9, swj, re-refits),
  r1 = w_nᵀ H w_n;
- the 128 × 128 tile error map (cell sums of E ∘ HE, (k/128, n/128));
- pre-refit su / sv (fp32, signs included: a refit can be redone later from the codes, H6);
- trajectory (f1 / f2 of the original-basis weight): pre-refit, post-refit, post-C9 (writers with in-job C9); tile CD's
  quantization-domain rel after LDLQ and per sweep (attn_qg); swj is a level pass (computable later from x1 / d1);
- chosen g_scale, overload counters (decoded |q| max, share of |q| ≥ 0.9 · max, share of regularized targets beyond
  max |q|, share of 16 × 16 tiles with error > 3 × the tensor median), proxy_err_2s (as today).

**Check:** CPU stand-in (`rxq/cpukern.py`, tiny model): Σ_n e1_n / Σ_n r1_n = the logged proxy to 1e-6 relative on
every tensor of every level; t-files bit-identical with the diagnostics on and off; `check_ladder`, `check_cpujob`,
rqah `rxq_e2e` pass with the patch. Reported: the extra bytes per level (measured on the gpubox jobs, scaled to 64
layers). Stamps: the settings stamp is unchanged while `quant.refit = true`; the code stamp changes (rxq sources).

**Runtime cost: none** (#130 changes fp16 scales only; #131 writes side files).
