## Item 1 · #117 · Residual-stream 128-block permutation ("block act order" for every residual reader)

**Idea.** One fixed permutation π of the 40 residual 128-blocks, applied to every residual reader's input columns
(GDN in_proj_qkv / in_proj_z, attention q / k / v, gate / up; also the bf16 / small in_proj_a / in_proj_b), every
writer's output rows (o_proj, GDN out_proj, down_proj), every residual-dimension norm gain, the embedding columns and
the head's input columns. Exact transform (RMSNorm is permutation-equivariant; whole 128-blocks keep the Hadamard
and A8 groups). Only the LDLQ order of the readers changes: exllamav3 quantizes the **last** stored 128-block first,
so "heavy-first in quantization order" = **heaviest blocks stored last** (w64 item 5: stored heaviest-first cost
+10% on o_proj).

### 1(a) · block-trace consistency (published calib H_in + bf16 weights)

For every reader tensor t of all 64 layers (GDN layers: ssm_qkv, ssm_z, ffn_gate, ffn_up; attention layers: attn_qg,
attn_k, attn_v, ffn_gate, ffn_up; 272 tensors), the per-block trace share of the quantizer's metric:
w_t(b) = Σ_{i∈b} su_i² (H_ii + δ) / Σ_i su_i² (H_ii + δ), with su the input-channel RMS of the row-normalised weight
(settle.P2's su; the 128-Hadamard inside a block keeps the block trace, so this is the block trace of the metric
LDLQ runs under), δ = adopted damping × mean diag H (0.005 input group, 0.025 gate / up). Reported too: the raw H
share (Σ_{i∈b} H_ii / tr H, shared by all readers of one H), the spread p90/p10 of w_t (#103: the act-order gain
tracks it).
**Consensus order:** score c(b) = parameter-weighted mean over the 272 tensors of log w_t(b); π stores the blocks by
ascending c (heaviest last). For agreement, each tensor is compared to the consensus **without its own layer**.
**Agreement rule (gate for 1(b)):** ρ_t = Spearman(w_t, leave-own-layer-out consensus). Rankings **agree** if
(i) median ρ_t ≥ 0.70, (ii) ≥ 80% of the 272 tensors have ρ_t ≥ 0.50, and (iii) every family's median ρ_t ≥ 0.50.
Reported, not deciding: median pairwise ρ across layers within a family and across families, by depth band; the raw
H version of the same; the per-layer spread.
If the rankings do not agree, 1(b) is not run (the lead may ask for it anyway).

## Item 2 · #118 · Knapsack pricing of the adopted settings

**Rules for item 2 written 2026-11-08 ~4:40 AM CST (10:40 UTC), before any item-2 number was computed** (the swj fit's
own `swiglu.jsonl` summaries and the levels' `proxy.jsonl` files were copied, not analysed).

**How the knapsack prices today (knap5 `KN_V3R`):** a V3 level's error = V2's hproxy error of the integer ours-K
option (one-sided, `tr(dW H dW^T)`, decoded containers) × DAMPF (attn q/k/v) × R8 / R4, fractional K placed by the
measured ladder positions (`KN_LEVELS`, ourq's one-sided `proxy_err`); swj enters as a measured whole-model KL factor
(`KN_SWJ_PTS`), H_out not at all. So no swj'd or H_out level's proxy is read today. The question is what goes wrong if
measured V3 proxies are read (the natural next step), and whether the V2-based anchors are off.

### 2(a) · one-sided proxy of swj'd vs plain gate / up (27B levels on iceman, CPU, niced)

Arms with fitted swj scales: K2.5 `base` (`/data/v3ladder2/K2.5`), `s1`, `bn`, `bns1`; K4 `base4`, `bn4` (scales
`/data/swj27/fit/<arm>/scales`). Per (arm, layer, gate / up): decode Ŵ (`exl3deq.py`, CPU), Ŵ_swj = rows × c with svh·c
rounded to f16 as `swjapply.py` stores it; e1 = Σ_r (w_r − ŵ_r) H (w_r − ŵ_r)ᵀ / Σ_r w_r H w_rᵀ under the calib H_in
(post_attention.input, undamped); e2 = the same with each row weighted by the Σdy² diagonal h_r
(`/data/swap27/hout-bn`, block-normalized; the two-sided proxy of w08-hprice). ρ1 = e1(swj) / e1(plain),
ρ2 = e2(swj) / e2(plain); per level the geometric mean over 64 layers × {gate, up} and over the arms at that K.
**Readout (rule):**
- swj makes the one-sided proxy **pessimistic** if geo ρ1 ≥ 1.03 at K2.5 while swj's measured KL ratio is < 1
  (0.935 / 0.932 at K2.5). Then any pricing that reads swj'd levels' one-sided proxies needs a per-level correction on
  gate / up of 1 / ρ1(K) (K2.5 and K4 measured; between them linear in K; outside, the nearest measured value), or must
  read the plain level's proxy and keep the KL factor (today's design). |log ρ1| < 0.03 at both K → no correction.
- ρ2 is reported as the alternative price (does a Σdy²-weighted proxy see swj's gain: ρ2 < 1?).
- K2 and K3: no swj'd 27B level exists (a fit needs the bf16 MLP-input pass on a card); not measured, reported as the
  interpolation / extrapolation above, flagged as such.
Also reported (same arithmetic, from the levels' own `proxy.jsonl`, no decode): the one-sided proxy of the H_out
levels against their plain twins (bn / base, bns1 / s1, bns2 / s2 at K2.5; bn4 / base4, bn4s1 / base4s1 at K4), next
to their measured KL ratios: H_out is now adopted at every level, so the same pessimism applies to every V3 level.

**What the conversion would have to permute** (recorded for any later revisit; the transform is exact):

  they move with the rows) and the writers' Σdy² H_out diagonal (reindexed);
- every residual-dimension norm gain: input_layernorm, post_attention_layernorm, model.norm (RMSNorm is permutation
  equivariant; q_norm / k_norm / linear_attn.norm act on head dims: unchanged);
- embed_tokens columns, lm_head input columns;
- vision: `model.visual.merger.linear_fc2` output rows and bias (the merger writes into the residual stream; no
  deepstack mergers in this checkpoint);
- MTP (if ever shipped): mtp.pre_fc_norm_embedding / pre_fc_norm_hidden, both halves of mtp.fc's input columns and its
  output rows, the MTP layer's readers / writers / norms, mtp.norm;
- DFlash2: the tap inputs of its fc (each of the 5 tapped 5120-wide hidden states, layers 5 / 19 / 33 / 47 / 61) and,
  since it shares the target's embedding and head, its whole residual dimension (fc output rows, its layers' readers /
  writers / norms); or it keeps unpermuted private copies of the embedding and head (decode VRAM).
- Stored EXL3 codes of non-readers can be permuted exactly without a requant (whole 16×16 tiles and 128-blocks of
  suh / svh move); only the readers need requantizing (that is where the order changes).
- **H_out / swj / C9:** H_out is output-side: the readers' Σdy² diagonal is over their own outputs and **needs no
  change**; only the writers' (residual-row) diagonal is reindexed. swj acts on gate / up output channels (not
  residual): unchanged. C9 / asym are per writer row: permuted with the rows. One catch for any 27B pair: the
  quantizer's random input signs are drawn per position, so a permuted quant is also a reseeded quant (27B seed-to-seed
  ~2.5% KLD) unless the sign vectors are permuted along with the columns.

- Per K: **ρ1 = 1.122 at K2.5, 1.056 at K4** → the one-sided proxy calls swj'd gate / up 12% (K2.5) / 6% (K4)
  worse while KL falls 6.5% / 1%. Rule: ≥ 1.03 at K2.5 with KL < 1 → **pessimistic**. If a pricing step ever reads a

- Mechanism (measured): the levels' svh sit at the per-row least-squares optimum of the one-sided proxy (refit: LS
  / plain = 0.99999); swj moves gate rows by +3.4% on average (up −1.6%) at K2.5 (+0.4% / −0.2% at K4), and any move off
  that optimum raises the proxy quadratically. The task's "~7–8%" is the size order; measured it is 12% at K2.5.
- The Σdy²-weighted proxy does not see swj's gain either (ρ2 1.13 / 1.08): swj's gain is the SwiGLU product, which no
  per-tensor linear proxy contains. So swj stays priced as a measured KL factor (`KN_SWJ_PTS`).
- **Does kfit2 / knap5 need the correction today? No.** knap5 reads plain-level proxies (ourq's `proxy.jsonl` is
  written before swjapply, so even the ladder's own files are pre-swj) and applies swj as a KL factor. The correction
  is needed only if a later step re-measures the shipped (swj'd) containers with hproxy and feeds those into kfit2
  (e.g. a V3 refit on V3 swaps): then use 1 / ρ1 above, or hproxy the pre-swj level.
- **The same holds for H_out, now adopted at every level** (from the levels' own proxy.jsonl): one-sided proxy of the
  H_out level / its plain twin = **1.076** (bn / base, bns1 / s1, bns2 / s2, all three 1.075–1.076) at K2.5 and
