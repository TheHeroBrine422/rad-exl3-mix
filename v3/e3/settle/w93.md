## Item 2 · #39-01 · Global gain on the order-fixed proxy with today's stack, held out

**Question.** #39 (w43, 11-06) found "flat, keep 1.0" on settle.py, whose LDLQ ran blocks first to last (exllamav3
runs last to first; the order bias was found 11-07, w64) and without H_out. Does a gain multiplier m on exllamav3's own
g_scale (v3.toml `quant.gain`, ourq `--gain`) beat 1.0 on today's quantizer objective?

**Proxy (`w93/settle93.py`):** w87's `settle87.py` unchanged (w77's held-out split: H_A quantizes, the fit-pool halves
H_B1 / H_B2 score; P2 transforms of one aligned 128-row block; exllamav3's scale search and ourq's drift; last-to-first
LDLQ; **two-sided** with the bn1920 H_out diagonal, σ_out 0.01; mul1x_i8; per-output-channel refit), plus
- gain: the trellis input scale is m · s_exl;
- the **#117 block order** (v3.toml `quant.block_order`, w84 `order82.json`) on the residual readers (ssm_qkv, ssm_z,
  attn_qg, attn_k, attn_v, ffn_gate_up): their LDLQ visits the input 128-blocks in the stored order, route B (exact
  on this proxy: its input Hadamard is block-diagonal by 128). Each reader also gets a natural-order twin at m = 1,
  reported only;
- σ = the current damping (inputs 0.005, rest 0.025).
Not emulated: tile CD (attn_qg rows are pre-CD), the su refit (as w43 / w87).
**Gate (before any gain row is read; `settle93.py selftest 11 attn_k 0`):** the identity block order through the
permuting path gives the natural path's codes bit for bit, and the #117 order changes some tiles. Fail → nothing read.

**Grid:** m ∈ {0.9, 0.95, 1.0, 1.05, 1.1}; levels i8 K2.5 and i8 K4; w43 / w87's 27 tensors (three per family, all
nine trunk families) × samples 0 / 1. Error = two-sided rel_X (X = B1, B2: held out). r(t, s, K, m) = rel_X(m) /
rel_X(1.0), paired inside one job.

**Rule.** `quant.gain` is one number for every tensor and K (rxq passes it globally), so the deciding test is global:
1. **Pick on B1:** m* = the grid value with the lowest geo mean of r_B1 over all 27 tensors × 2 samples × both K.
2. **Judge on B2:** g(K) = 1 − geo mean of r_B2(m*) over 27 × 2 at that K. A tensor *agrees* at K if its geo mean of
   r_B2(m*) over the 2 samples is < 1.
3. **Propose m*** (for the lead; v3.toml is not edited here) only if m* ≠ 1.0, **g ≥ 1 % at both K2.5 and K4** (#39's
   margin: ≥ 1 % at ≥ 2 K) and **≥ 18 / 27 tensors agree at each K**. Otherwise: **keep 1.0**.
4. **Per family (#39's original table form, reported; a per-family gain would need an rxq change):** per (family, K),
   m*_f on B1 (3 tensors × 2 samples), g_f on B2; an entry qualifies if g_f ≥ 1 % and every tensor of the family has
   r_B2(m*_f) ≤ 1.00 on both samples; a family "would get a table" if it qualifies at both K. Any such family is
   reported as a proposal with the rxq change it needs, not adopted.
5. Edge: if m* (global, or a qualifying family's m*_f) is 0.9 or 1.1, the grid is extended by one step (0.85 / 1.15)
   for the tensors concerned before the verdict is read; the rule is then applied to the extended grid.
Reported, not deciding: the curves on B (pooled), on A (in-sample), one-sided rel1 and rel before the refit; m* picked
on B2 against B1's; per family and per K; the readers' #117 order vs natural order at m = 1 (expected ≤ ~0.5 %).
**Runtime cost: none** (quantizer setting: same format, kernels, bpw).
