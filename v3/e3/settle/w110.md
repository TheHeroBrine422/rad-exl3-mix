## Statistics

For an arm A vs its mix's T: r = KL(A) / KL(T) as a ratio of position sums.
- **pooled r** over both parts (`w84/pair84.py`: document bootstrap stratified by part, 2000 resamples, seed 0) with
  its 95% interval and z; **part r** (xcorp, calib) separately;
- **per-document** (`eval/docstat/docstat.py` per part and both): per-document geo-mean and median of r, share of
  documents better, top-document share of T's KL, the ratio without documents holding > 20% of a part's KL.

`pass(A)` := pooled r ≤ 0.99 **and** per-document geo (both parts) ≤ 0.99 **and** xcorp-part r < 1 **and**
calib-part r < 1. `worse(A)` := pooled r ≥ 1.00 or per-document geo ≥ 1.00.

## Rule

**C9.** Candidates X ∈ {off, e3}, swj on.
1. **Global change** (every tested K, i.e. the sub-1 / 1.x levels): X replaces cur if pass(X) in M1 **and**
   pass(XX) in M2 (`oo` / `ee`). If both off and e3 qualify, the one with the lower geo-mean of the two mixes' pooled r.
2. **K-band split** (only if no global change). The three measurement points are K0.5 (M2 band arm `Xc`), K1 (M1)
   and K1.5 (M2 band arm `cX`). "X below K1": pass(Xc) **and not** pass(X in M1) **and** worse(cX). "X from K1 up":
   pass(X in M1) **and** pass(cX) **and** worse(Xc). Any other pattern (e.g. a pass at K0.5 that M1 or `cX` does not
   bracket) is reported, not adopted. The boundary between K0.5 and K1 (K0.625–0.875) is unmeasured: a "below K1"
   split is proposed as "X at K ≤ 0.875" with that caveat.
3. Otherwise C9 stays as today.

**swj.** Candidate "swj off" (C9 cur), same structure with `n` for off: global if pass(off in M1) and pass(`nn` in
M2) → propose `post.swj.k_min` above 1.5 (at least 2: no level in between was tested); band "off below K1" if pass(`ns`)
and not pass(off in M1) and worse(`sn`) → propose `k_min = 1` (sub-1 levels without swj). Otherwise swj stays at
every level ≤ K4.5.

**Per mix (reported, the task's form):** the best C9 setting and swj on / off by pooled r with both parts agreeing in
sign, and the full 3 × 2 table with the C9 × swj interaction (r(X, off) / (r(X, on) · r(cur, off))).

**Confounder check (AGENTS.md):** any result ≥ 3× the 0.8B's magnitude or against its direction is "unverified"
until G2 holds (identical outside the arm) and the sign holds on both parts; a second slice is the lead's call.

**Runtime cost: none** (C9 and swj fold into svh / suh; same format and kernels). Card cost of the experiment: three
level quants (~3–4 card-h with #142's fusion), one 3-source swj pass (~1.7 card-h), 18 mixes × 2 parts scored
(~1.5 card-h); packs on gpubox CPU under conv.lock + ram.lock exclusive.
