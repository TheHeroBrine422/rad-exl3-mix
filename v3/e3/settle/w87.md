## Proxy (`w87/settle87.py`)

w77's `settle77.py` setup unchanged (P2 transforms on one aligned 128-row output block, exllamav3's scale search with
ourq's drift, LDLQ order last-block-first, mul1x_i4 / mul1x_i8, per-output-channel refit under H_A + δI; the

quantizer never saw) and in-sample H_A), same 27 tensors × samples 0 / 1, same row cache and scale searches (w77's
files; K4 scale searches are new, same code). Added:

- **Two-sided LDLQ**, exllamav3's `ldlq_2hess` + `prepare_H_out` (fork eb5a2139): H_out = diag(h) (bn1920) is taken
  into the rotated basis as P S_v diag(h) S_v P (S_v = output signs × output-channel RMS scales, P = the proxy's
  128-Hadamard), divided by its mean diagonal **over the whole tensor** (all N rows, not just the sample), + σ_out
  0.01 I, block-LDL (16). Because P is block-diagonal by 128, the rotated H_out is block-diagonal by 128 and the
  two-sided LDLQ of one 128-row block is **exactly** the production computation restricted to that block (no
  approximation from sampling rows). Tile order: input blocks last to first, output tiles last to first inside a
  block (any order consistent with ldlq_2hess's anti-diagonals gives identical results).
- **Two-sided error** in the original basis: e_X = Σ_i h_i d_i H_X d_iᵀ, rel_X = e_X / Σ_i h_i w_i H_X w_iᵀ (i over
  the block's 128 output channels; d after the per-channel refit, whose optimum does not depend on a diagonal H_out).
  This is the objective the quantizer now serves. H_out is the quantizer's own (the output side is in-sample; it is
  not tuned here, and the held-out question concerns H_in, whose estimation noise damping regularizes). The one-sided
  rel of the same quantization (unweighted) is also stored, reported only.
