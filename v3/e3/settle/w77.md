## Held-out design (why it differs from damp3's split halves)

Quantizing and scoring on the same Hessian always favours less damping (#38 damp3). damp3 split 2.4M tokens into two
1.2M halves (layers 3 / 10 / 43, no down_proj: on iceman, off limits now). Here, with no GPU:
- **B = 160k tokens of fit-pool calib documents** (pools-v3; whole documents of 256–4,096 tokens, shuffled with seed
  77, alternately assigned to **B1** and **B2**, ~80k tokens each). `hb77.py` runs the bf16 checkpoint (upcast to fp32;
  HF transformers' Qwen3_5 decoder layers, one layer in memory at a time) over each document as one sequence and sums
  x xᵀ for the 26 input groups the 27 tensors need, giving **H_B1, H_B2**.
- The published calib Hessian is the sum over every calib token (metadata rows 10,259,331 = the calib token stream
  `tokens.i32` exactly), so **H_A = H_pub − H_B1 − H_B2** is the Hessian of the other ~10.1M tokens: the quantizer's
  sample is about the ladder's size (damp3's was 1.2M, which overstates the need for damping).
- The quantizer (LDLQ metric with damping, exllamav3's per-output-channel refit) sees **H_A only**. The error
  rel = tr(dW H dW^T) / tr(W H W^T) is reported under **H_B1, H_B2, B = B1 + B2** (held out), H_A (in-sample) and H_pub.
- Checks before any rel is read: every job's row count equals its planned token count; H_A has no non-positive
  diagonal entry; per group, mean diag(H_B) / rows_B against mean diag(H_pub) / rows_pub (a different document sample,
  so not exactly 1: reported, and a ratio outside [0.5, 2] stops the run for a diagnosis).
- Pools: B is fit-pool data used for a quantizer hyperparameter (the ledger needs a line: role fit-hessian, collection
  `w77-hb`; the lead adds it, I do not edit shared files).

## Rule (#114, per cell = (band, family); also per (band, group))

r(t, s, ℓ, σ) = rel_X(σ) / rel_X(σ_cur(t, ℓ)), X a scoring Hessian; paired (same rows, signs, scale, levels).
1. **Pick on B1:** σ*(cell) = the grid value with the lowest geometric mean of r_B1 over the cell's tensors × 2 samples
   × levels in the band.
2. **Judge on B2:** gain g = 1 − geo mean of r_B2(σ*) over the same set. A tensor *agrees* if its own geo mean of
   r_B2(σ*) over samples × levels is < 1.
3. **Adopt** σ* for that (band, family) if σ* ≠ σ_cur, **g ≥ 2%** and **≥ ⅔ of the cell's tensors agree** (2 of 3).
   Otherwise the current value stays for that cell. Group cells (inputs: 5 families, 15 tensors; rest: 4, 12) by the
   same rule (≥ 10 / 15, ≥ 8 / 12 agree) — a group-level pass would mean "one value per group, but a different one at
   this level".
4. σ* on a grid end (0.0025 or 0.2) is flagged; an adopted end value needs the grid extended before use.
5. Reported, not deciding: the **curve** per cell on B (geo r_B per σ: shape, e.g. flat / U / monotone, and the width
   within 1% of the minimum), the same on H_A (in-sample: its bias toward less damping) and H_pub; per-level curves in
   the sub-1 band; B1-pick vs B2-pick agreement (noise of the held-out sample); per-tensor σ* spread.
