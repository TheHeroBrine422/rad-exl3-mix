## Item 0 · settle.py in exllamav3's LDLQ order (gate for every CPU result here)

settle.py (`trellis.ldlq_trellis`) quantizes the 16-column input blocks first to last; exllamav3's `ldlq()` goes last
to first (`for j in range(size_k, 0, -buf_size_k)`, then `for bj in range(buf_size_k, 0, -16)`). w50 found this is
why settle.py's tile-CD gain on 3:attn_qg was ~4× the GPU's. **`settle64.py`** = settle.py with
`SETTLE_ORDER=rev` (default): the 16-column blocks are permuted to reverse order **with each block's own column order
kept** (so every 16×16 tile is the same tile as before), LDLQ runs in that order under the permuted metric, and the
codes are permuted back (scoring is permutation-invariant). Block-LDLQ in a block order is exactly GPTQ in that
order, so this is exllamav3's `ldlq()` up to rounding. CD sweeps (mode cd) stay ascending, as the GPU's `tilecd.py`.
`SETTLE_ORDER=fwd` reproduces settle.py bit for bit (self-check before launch).

**Check (rule):** mode cd (3 sweeps, m = 1, adopted damping, mul1_i8) on 27B attn_qg layers 3 / 19 / 43, K2 / 3 / 4,
samples 0 and 1, i.e. the blocks of w50's GPU table (`v3/e3/tilecd/README.md`, check 2, "GPU same blocks s0 / s1").
r = cd3 ÷ LDLQ (rel after the refit). **PASS** if for each of the 9 (layer, K) the geometric mean over the two samples
is within **±0.5%** of the GPU's same-block geometric mean. FAIL → no settle64 result below is used; the miss is
reported and diagnosed before anything else.
Reported: rev ÷ fwd LDLQ error per tensor (how much the order alone moves the proxy).

## Item 1 · #111 · Writers' asym under knapsack mixes (0.8B KL, 4070)

**Question.** #87's writers asym (ρ 0) is fitted to the exact rounding of the writer's siblings (q/k/v, GDN qkv/z,
gate/up). #87-07 showed it does not survive a sibling *seed* change (K3 1.049, K2 1.026: worse than none). In a
knap4 mix the siblings sit at another K than the writer. Does a **per-level** asym writer (fitted on its own level's
siblings) still help there?

**Setup** (the adopted 0.8B stack, d1112's `k{K}st` recipe on today's code): trunk **edy** (`q2b.sh 08 K i8`, c18
Σdy² H_out, σ_out 0.01, seed offset s) → **swj** (`swiglufit.py`, g08-fit1) → writers. Siblings at level K_s are
the edy + swj quant of K_s (102 non-writer tensors); writers (48, `only.txt`) at level K_w. Cells
(K_w, K_s) = **A (3, 2.5)**, **B (3, 3.5)**, **C (2, 2.5)**; seeds s = 0, 1, 2 (the siblings and writers of a cell
share the seed offset, as the ladder's levels share theirs). Arms per (cell, seed):
- **xc** plain writers at K_w (d1112's `q-k{K_w}ste{_s}` shards = the edy writers) on the K_s siblings;
- **xs** per-level asym writers at K_w (d1112's `q-k{K_w}st{_s}` shards: fitted on the K_w level's own swj siblings)
  on the K_s siblings: what shipping per-level asym into a mix does;
- **xm** asym writers at K_w refitted on the mix's siblings: moments (`moments.py --mode sib`, qad, 1M tokens,
  seed 0, through the K_s siblings; a writer's sib-mode moments depend only on its own block's siblings), G, F
  (`mkdenoise.py` λ 1 ρ 0), the 48 writers requantized at K_w (`--only`, `--hin G --wpre F`, same H_out and seed):
  the per-mix writer pass (fallback a).
Writers of every arm are taken from their shards by `deq.py` (`v3/yaqa/asym27/deq.py`, the plugin's decode: fp16
scales, as shipped), so xc / xs / xm are built the same way; siblings are the swj `--save-wq` weights.
Scored with `evalq.py` on **g08-search1** (deciding) and **g08-verify1** (check, read once).

**Gate (seed 0, before any cell is scored):** the fresh K3 edy + swj siblings with the decoded d1112 `k3st` writers
must reproduce d1112's `k3st` search KLD within ±1% (and the decoded `k3ste` writers d1112's `k3ste_swj` within
±1%). Fail → stop, report, no cell is read.

**Statistics.** Per (cell, seed): r_x = KL(xs) / KL(xc), r_m = KL(xm) / KL(xc) (paired, `pcmp.py`, per-document z).
geo = geometric mean over the 3 seeds. Kept share κ = ln(geo r_x) / ln(geo r_m) (defined when geo r_m < 0.99).

**Rule (per-level asym usable in knap4 mixes?):**
- **SURVIVES:** in every cell, geo r_x ≤ 0.99, every seed r_x < 1, and κ ≥ 0.5 → per-level asym writers ship into
  mixes as they are (no per-mix pass).
- **FAILS:** geo r_x ≥ 1.00 in any cell → per-level asym must not be shipped next to siblings of another K. The lead
  chooses between (a) a per-mix writer pass (xm says what it delivers: ~4–5 card-h per mix at 27B, ASYM27.md),
  (b) tying writer K to its siblings' K in knap4, (c) plain writers wherever siblings differ.
- **PARTIAL:** anything else: reported with κ per cell; the lead decides (e.g. asym only where K_s = K_w).
- Reported, not deciding: verify ratios (flag if verify keeps < ⅔ of the search gain in a cell), r_m per cell (does
  a per-mix pass recover the uniform-level gain, d1112's k{K}st / k{K}ste_swj ≈ 0.86 K3 / 0.83 K2?), the K_s = K_w
  gate pair as the matched reference.
Fallback (c) of w61 (a fixed-code svh / suh refit of the writers to the asym target per mix) is **not run**: it needs
the per-mix moments too, which are the cost of fallback (a) at 27B (moments 2.5–3 card-h vs 20 min of writer quant),
so it would not be cheaper.
**Runtime cost: none** (quantizer only: same format, kernels, bpw).

## Item 2 · #109 · Per-rate int8 factor ("clipped i8") at K6.5–8 (CPU, settle64)

**Question.** i8 (`q = clamp(rint(v / s4), ±127)`, s4 = 4 k_inv, one int8 step = 4 bytesum units) costs ×1.27 vs
mul1 at K7 and ×1.7–4.5 above (r94b, w46 item 5: real quants agree). The quantizer's own g_scale keeps tiles at
~0.71–0.83 codebook sd, so the outer int8 states are rarely used. Does a per-rate factor α (finer int8 step s4 / α,
values beyond ±127 steps clipped) close the gap?

**Codebooks** (`settle64.py`, from cbx.py's exact fork values): `mul1x` (reference), `mul1x_i8` (α = 1, today's i8),
`mul1x_i8c<α>` = fp16(clamp(rint(v · α / s4), −127, 127) · s4 / α) for α ∈ {1.15, 1.33, 1.5, 1.75, 2}. The g_scale
search runs with each codebook (it absorbs the 1/α), m = 1 (the quantizer's own scale), adopted damping, rel after the
per-output-channel refit. Tensors: r94b's 10 (`r94b/jobs.txt`); K ∈ {6.5, 7, 7.25, 7.5, 8}; samples 0 and 1.
r = rel(codebook) / rel(mul1x) per (tensor, K, sample).

**Rule:** per K, α*(K) = the α (including 1) with the lowest median r over the 10 tensors on **sample 0**; judged on
**sample 1** with that α*:
- **closes the K7 gap** if at K7 median r ≤ **1.05** and p90 ≤ 1.10, and at K6.5 median r(α*(6.5)) ≤ r(α = 1)
  (no harm where i8 already works) → recommend a per-rate α table for the i8 levels (a codebook / decode change:
  the lead and user decide, as the mul1i8 decision of 11-06), after **one 4070 real-quant check** (ourq with a
  patched private exllamav3 build, 0.8B layers 6 + 7 as w46 item 5, rel from `--save-wq`) agreeing within ±5%.
- **i8 K7.25 / 7.5 / 7.75 joins** (#94's rule, unchanged): median r ≤ 1.10 and p90 ≤ 1.20 at that K with α*.
- **negative** otherwise (K7 median r > 1.10 at every α): i8 stays as priced (×1.27 at K7, mul1 above).
- Reported: r per α and K (both samples, pooled), the equal-error mul1 K (log-interpolated on mul1x's K curve), and
  plain i8 / mul1 on the order-fixed proxy next to r94b's ×1.018 / ×1.27 / ×1.69 / ×2.33 / ×4.48.
**Runtime cost: decode** (and prefill only through the decode pass): the int8 value becomes a multiply + round +
clamp per rate (or a 1,021-entry table per rate) instead of the fixed closed form. If it passes, the kernel cost is
priced before adoption (two-pass decode: expected ~0 on prefill).

## Item 3 · #112 · C9 gain refit (α) for the writers after asym (0.8B KL, 4070)

**Question.** exllamav3's `refit_scales` is a least-squares fit: each row ends with |q|²_M = |t|²_M (1 − e_n) in its
fit metric M and target t. C9 (#85) un-shrinks it per row, c_n *= (1 − e_n)^(−α/2), one α per family (α = 1 restores
the row's norm). It paid on plain 0.8B quants (K3 0.973, K2 0.911, α* at the grid edge 2). #85-02's "C9 on top of
swj" applied α fitted without swj, and measured the writers' e_n against W instead of W·F: it does not count. After
asym the writers are refit by LS again (to W·F under G), so the shrink comes back.

**Setup.** Uniform levels K ∈ {2, 3}, seeds 0, 1, 2. Siblings: fresh edy + swj at K (as item 1). Two stacks:
- **st** (the adopted stack): asym writers decoded from d1112's `q-k{K}st{_s}`; e_n against **t = W·F, M = G** of
  that level (moments through the level's swj siblings + mkdenoise λ 1 ρ 0, as d1112; recomputed here);
- **pl** (no asym): plain edy writers decoded from `q-k{K}ste{_s}`; e_n against t = W, M = calib H_in.
e_n is computed raw (undamped M), as C9. Families: **attn_o** (o_proj), **gdn_out** (out_proj), **down** (down_proj).
`c9w.py` applies the multipliers to the decoded writer weights (codes unchanged; the factor folds into svh).

**Screen (seed 0, g08-fit1 = fit pool):** per (K, stack, family) α ∈ {−1, 1, 2, 3} with the other families at 0,
plus α = 0. α* per family by C9's pick rule (#85, unchanged): quadratic through the paired fit diffs at
(−1, 0, 1, 2, 3), vertex clipped to [−1, 3] (the best measured point if concave), rounded to 0.05; α* = 0 unless the
best measured point beats α = 0 at z ≤ −2 on fit.
**Confirm:** the combined α* (per K, stack) on seeds 0, 1, 2, search (deciding) and verify (check), paired with the
same stack at α = 0.

**Rule (st stack, the adoption question):**
- **PASS → 27B fixed-code pair** (the writers' svh after asym on a K2.5 level; no requant, ~1 card-h, lead
  schedules on gpubox): at both K, search geo ≤ **0.99**, every seed < 1, and verify keeps ≥ ⅔ of the search gain
  (mean log ratio over seeds on verify ≤ ⅔ × the search one).
- **FAIL:** search geo > 0.995 at both K, or α* = 0 for every family at both K → closed for the writers after asym.
- **PARTIAL:** anything else (e.g. one K passes): reported; a per-K α only goes on with the lead's call.
- The **pl** stack is read with the same thresholds and reported: it is the answer if item 1 sends plain writers into
  mixes. Reported too: α* per family and K, the per-row multiplier spread, the fit-slice curves.
**Runtime cost: none** (svh multipliers: same format, kernels, bpw).

## Item 4 · #110 · bf16 (row, 128) scales on attn_output / ssm_out from K5 (CPU, settle64)

**Question.** IQR-V4 measured −1…−6% KL at 4–6 bit on this model and board with a bf16 scale per (row, 128 inputs) on
the head-fed writers. #95-02 rejected it (×1.18–1.29 *worse* at equal bits) on trellis3.py, whose LDLQ order is
reversed vs exllamav3's and whose scale handling is not exllamav3's. Retest on settle64 (exllamav3's order,
transforms, g_scale and refit).

**Where the scales live.** In the stored (regularized) domain: one scale per stored row (output channel after the
output Hadamard) and 128-column block of W_hat (= one input-Hadamard block; a per-block scalar commutes with the
block Hadamard and su). Cost **0.125 bpw** on these tensors (16 bits / 128 weights) → compared at **equal bits**
against plain K: variants at K − 0.125 (qtf2's 1/8 step) vs plain at K.

**Arms** (one job = one tensor, K, sample; all arms paired on the same rows, signs and sample):
- `plain@K` and `plain@K−⅛` (the reference and the bits-for-quality slope);
- `post@K−⅛`: plain codes at K − ⅛, then the (row, block) scales refit jointly per row under the LDLQ metric (closed
  form, rounded to bf16) before the per-output-channel refit (#95-02's joint solve, in the right domain and order);
- `pre@K−⅛`: IQR's route: before the trellis, each (row, block) of the transformed weight is divided by its RMS
  (bf16), the global g_scale is searched on the normalised tensor, LDLQ quantizes the normalised target block by
  block (feedback in the unscaled domain), codes × scale;
- `prepost@K−⅛`: pre, then the post refit.
Tensors: attn_output L3 / 19 / 43 / 63, ssm_out L2 / 10 / 30 / 50 / 62 (#95-02's); K ∈ {5, 6} deciding, K4 and K7
reported; samples 0 and 1. Statistic per tensor: geometric mean over the two samples of
r = rel(variant @ K−⅛) / rel(plain @ K).

**Rule (#95-02's, unchanged, plus a no-harm condition):** a variant is **worth a real check** if at K5 or K6 its
median r over the family's tensors is ≤ **0.97 on both attn_output and ssm_out**, and no tensor has r > 1.00 on
either sample at that K. Then: one 4070 real-quant check on a 27B layer (a private ourq / exllamav3 build) and the
kernel pricing (it adds a scale per (row, 128) in the K loop: prefill and decode). **Fail** otherwise: the IQR
result does not carry over to EXL3 (its su / sv scales already do most of this work), closed for V3.
Reported: r at K4 / K7, rel(plain@K−⅛) / rel(plain@K) (what ⅛ bit is worth), the spread of the fitted scales.
**Runtime cost: prefill + decode** if adopted (a per-(row, 128) scale in the K loop on ~8% of trunk weights).
