## Item 2 · 0.8B drift / variance split (4070)

**Levels:** the existing 6-seed plain 0.8B levels `/data/yb/q-k2base{,_s1..s5}` and `q-k3base{,_s1..s5}` (ourq i8,
qtf2, scaled Hessian, σ 0.025 / 0.005, seed offsets 0–5; dequantized `wq/`, fp16 → bf16 on load as `evalq.py`). No
requant. **Slice:** g08-search1 (173 docs, 196,542 rows), scored with `evalq.py`'s arithmetic (the challenge's
513-cell KL: golden top-512 + tail; gen-masked).
- **Gate G3:** each seed's per-row KL equals the stored `/data/yb/k{2,3}base*.f32` rows (max |Δ| ≤ 1e-6, else the
  dump path is wrong and nothing below is read).

Per scored row t and seed s: Δ_s = log q_s − log p̃ on the 513 cells (p̃ = the golden's renormalised cells).
b̂ = mean_s Δ_s. KL_single = mean_s KL(p̃ ‖ q_s); KL_avg = KL(p̃ ‖ q̄), q̄ ∝ p̃ · exp(b̂) (the seed-averaged logits).
With the quadratic model KL ≈ ½ Var_p̃(Δ): KL_single = A + B, KL_avg = A + B/S (A coherent, B seed variance, S = 6),
so **A = KL_avg − (KL_single − KL_avg)/(S − 1)** and the **coherent share c = A / KL_single** (sums over gen rows;
document bootstrap 95% CI). Also reported: the same split from the variances directly (Var_p̃(b̂) − V_ξ/(S−1)), the
uncorrected ratio KL_avg / KL_single (D4's number), per-category c, and c on the top-1% KL rows.

**Bias oracle (item 2b, what a bias field can actually take):** per quantized linear l, β_l = (W_l − Ŵ_l) μ_l, μ_l =
the mean input of linear l in the quantized model (that seed) over the scored positions of the first 32,768 rows of
g08-fit1 (fit pool); all β_l added at once as output biases (one pass, not sequential); scored on g08-search1. Seeds
s0–s2 at K2 and K3; r = KL(bias) / KL(plain) per seed.

**Rule (item 2):**
- c < 0.10 at both K → **NEGATIVE** (a bias field can fix at most a tenth of the KL).
- c ≥ 0.10 at K2 or K3 **and** the bias oracle's geo r ≤ 0.99 at that K with all 3 seeds < 1 → **POSITIVE**.
- c ≥ 0.10 but the oracle misses → **NEGATIVE for a field** ("coherent but not bias-shaped"; zero-byte coherent fixes
  such as norm gains stay open, reported).

## Item B6 · damping normalization (lead note 11-08 "1:00 PM CST"; rule written 12:52 PM CST / 18:52 UTC by the VM clock, before any B6 data; jobs started 18:54 UTC)

Merge B6 (I001909): damping is δ = σ × mean diag(H) (exllamav3's `finalize_capture_H`, on the unrotated H), and the
massive channel inflates that mean at early layer inputs (step 0 below: mean / median of the layer-input diagonal 9.1
at L2, 28.1 at L3, 27.1 at L7, 5.2 at L10, 18.9 at L11; 2.8 at L50, 1.8 at L63), so every normal channel of an early
reader may be over-damped. We do not regularize channel 3994 itself; only the normalization changes.

**Proxy:** w87's `settle87.py` unchanged (w77's held-out design: quantizer sees H_A = H_pub − H_B1 − H_B2; two-sided
LDLQ with the bn1920 H_out diagonal, σ_out 0.01; per-output-channel refit under H_A + δI; error scored two-sided on
the held-out H_B1 / H_B2), wrapped by `w91/settle91.py`, which only changes δ: **δ = σ_cur × N(H_A)** with
- N_mean = mean diag(H_A) (today),
- N_med = median diag(H_A),
- N_trl = (tr H_A − λ_max(H_A)) / (n − 1) (the mean without the top eigen-direction),
implemented as σ_eff = σ_cur × N / N_mean through settle87's own σ path (LDLQ metric and refit both see the same δ).
σ_cur as today (inputs 0.005 at K ≥ 1, 0.05 at K0.5).
**Tensors** (w77's held-out Hessians exist for these): early layer-input readers L2 ssm_qkv, L3 attn_qg, L7 attn_v,
L10 ssm_z, L11 attn_k; late L50 ssm_qkv, L63 attn_k; samples 0 / 1. **Levels:** i8 K2.5 and i8 K3 (deciding);
i4 K1 and i4 K0.5 (reported only).

**Rule (B6, w87's arithmetic):** r(t, s, ℓ, n) = rel_X(n) / rel_X(mean), paired (same rows, signs, scale, level).
1. **Pick on B1:** n* ∈ {med, trl} with the lower geo mean of r_B1 over the 7 tensors × 2 samples × {K2.5, K3}.
2. **Judge on B2:** g = 1 − geo mean of r_B2(n*) over the same set; a tensor agrees if its geo r_B2(n*) over samples
   × levels is < 1.
3. **WIN** iff g ≥ 2% and ≥ ⅔ of the 7 tensors agree (≥ 5). Otherwise **mean normalization stands**.
4. Reported: the early-5 and late-2 subsets separately, each level, both alternatives, the in-sample H_A curves, the
   effective σ ratio N / N_mean per tensor, the K1 / K0.5 cells.
5. A WIN triggers the lead's follow-up (σ re-sweep under the winning normalization, w87's reduced grid σ 0.001–0.2 ×
   K0.125 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 2.5 / 4, H_out on, held out, rule as #114; a proposed σ table, no v3.toml edit).

### Item 3 · design note (triggered by item 2): `w91/exl3i-fields.md`

The note's conclusion: **reserve no exl3i field.** A bias is computed from fixed codes after quantization, so it never
forces a ladder requant. The clean form is an optional per-linear bias *weight* in the qwen35_exl3 architecture plugin,
which leaves the exl3i encoding and every existing container byte-identical. It can land after the freeze if a
27B check passes. Sparse exact rows / side-car planes: no evidence (item 1 DROP), not reserved. Full spec, selftest,
costs and the 27B check are in the note.
