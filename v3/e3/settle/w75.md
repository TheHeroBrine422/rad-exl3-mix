## #112-01 · gdn_out α beyond 3 (0.8B, 4070)

**Why.** w64 item 3 (#112) passed on both stacks, almost all from gdn_out at α = 3, the grid edge (fit ratios still
falling at 3: st K2 0.969 → 0.960 from α 2 → 3). Where does the curve turn?

**Arms.** Stacks st (u: asym writers, e_n against W·F under G of the level) and pl (p: plain writers, e_n against W
under calib H_in), K ∈ {3, 2}; w64's base mixes (`mix-{u,p}{K}{x}`, α = 0) and c9w's arithmetic unchanged
(`c9w75.py` = w64's `c9w.py` + an optional `--cmax`, off unless stated). Other families at w64's α*
(u3: attn_o 0, down 0; u2, p3, p2: attn_o −1, down 0); gdn_out g ∈ {3, 4, 5, 6, 8}.

**Screen (seed 0, g08-fit1 = fit pool).** Each arm scored on fit and paired (pcmp.py) with w64's fit base
`fit-{st}{K}` (α = 0 everywhere) and with the g = 3 arm.
**Pick (C9's rule transcribed to the extension).** Least-squares quadratic through the five (g, paired fit diff vs
α = 0) points; if convex, its vertex clipped to [3, 8], else the best measured g; rounded to 0.25. α*_new = 3 unless the
best measured g > 3 beats the g = 3 arm at z ≤ −2 on fit.
**Confirm** (each (stack, K) with α*_new ≠ 3): the arm at α*_new on seeds 0–2, g08-search1 (deciding) and g08-verify1
(check), paired with w64's confirm arm of the same seed (g = 3: `{st}{K}{x}c9`, its scores reused) and reported also
against α = 0.

**Rule (per stack and K; st and pl read separately; pl feeds the 27B, as asym27 has not passed):**
- **RAISE:** search geo(α*_new / g 3) ≤ **0.995**, every seed < 1, and verify keeps ≥ ⅔ of the search gain (mean
  log ratio over seeds on verify ≤ ⅔ × the search one) → gdn_out α* at that K becomes α*_new.
- **KEEP 3:** anything else, including α*_new = 3 from the screen.
- **27B second arm (#112-02 arm B):** if pl RAISEs at ≥ 1 K: α_27 = mean of pl's α* at K2 and K3 (a K that keeps
  counts as 3), rounded to 0.5. Otherwise no arm B.
- **Reported, not deciding:** the fit curve per (stack, K); the per-row multiplier spread at every g (per family: mean,
  sd, max of c, the share of rows with c > 1.5 and > 2, e_n max, clipped rows). **Clamp check:** if any row's c > 2 at
  α*_new, one more seed-0 fit arm with c clamped to ≤ 2 (`--cmax 2`), paired with the unclamped arm (report only: says
  whether the lead needs a clamp, not adopted from this run).
**Runtime cost: none** (svh multipliers: same format, kernels, bpw).

## #112-02 · C9 on the writers, 27B fixed-code pair (gpubox card 0)

**Level.** The adopted 27B stack at K2.5 with plain writers: the swj levels `/data/swj27/lv/baseswj` (rotation seed 0)
and `/data/swj27/lv/s1swj` (seed 1) (K2.5, qtf2, scaled H, σ 0.025 / 0.005, i8, no H_out; swj only edits gate / up
svh, SWJ27.md). Plain writers because asym27 (#105) has not run (`/data/asym27`: dry run only) and #111 failed in
mixes.
**Arms** (per seed: `<seed>c9a`, `<seed>c9b`):
- **A:** attn_o (16 o_proj) α −1, gdn_out (48 out_proj) α 3, down 0 (the 0.8B pl α* at K2 and K3).
- **B:** only if #112-01 gives α_27 (rule above): gdn_out α_27, attn_o −1, down 0.
**Multipliers** (`c9e27.py`, card 0, minutes): c9w's arithmetic at 27B: Q = the level's writer decoded as shipped
(`deq.py`, f16 suh / svh), t = W (bf16 checkpoint), M = the published calib H_in of the writer's group (raw, undamped),
e_n = (q_n − t_n) M (q_n − t_n)ᵀ / t_n M t_nᵀ clipped to [0, 0.5], c_n = (1 − e_n)^(−α/2).
**Container** (`swjapply.py`, unchanged): level-c9 = the level with svh *= c on the 64 writers (f16 as stored); codes,
suh and every other tensor byte for byte. Packed and mixed with swap27's `packmixk` (exl3i mul1i8 rate 2.5 into V2
3.00, install-116 home: the controls' build), census.
**Identity gate (before any score).** `radiff.py`: every weight of the arm's mix vs the control mix
(`/data/swj27/mix-{baseswj,s1swj}.rad`): identical name set, shape, encoding struct and plane bytes, **except** the
svh plane of exactly the changed writers; and for those, decoded svh_arm / svh_ctl = c within f16 rounding
(|f16(svh·c) − svh_arm| = 0 for ≥ 99.9% of rows, ≤ 1 ulp all). Fail → that arm is not scored; reported.
**Controls.** `baseswj` / `s1swj` mixes as they are, **re-scored in this run** on both slices (same binary and home as
the arms); the fresh scores are the pair's control; their agreement with the stored scores (max |row diff|) is
reported.
**Scoring.** golden-search27 (search pool, 103 docs, 123,460 positions; **deciding**) and golden-verify27 (verify pool;
**check**, read once, after the decision), bf16 KV, swap27's `score`; `tools/paired.py` (per-document bootstrap).
r_i = KLD(arm) / KLD(control), i ∈ {base, s1}; g = √(r_base · r_s1).
**Rule (arm A; arm B by the same rule, and B vs A below):**
- **PASS → adopt C9 on the writers as a per-level step after swj** (fixed codes, minutes per level): g ≤ **0.99**, both
  r_i < 1 with z ≤ −2, and on verify both r_i < 1 and verify keeps ≥ ⅔ of the search gain (mean log ratio).
- **FAIL:** g ≥ 1.00 or any search r_i ≥ 1.
- **PARTIAL:** anything else (e.g. real but < 1%): reported; the lead decides.
- **Flags (reported with the verdict, lead looks before adopting):** a p99 KLD ratio > 1.01 or a top-1 drop > 0.1 pt
  on either slice.
- **B vs A:** B preferred if search geo(B / A) ≤ 0.995 with both < 1, and verify geo(B / A) < 1.
- Reported: z, per-document SE, the multiplier spread per family and layer band, e_n mean by family vs the 0.8B's.
Two seed pairs (AGENTS: ≥ 2). Fixed-code pairs have no quantization noise in the contrast; the two seeds decide
whether the setting transfers. No third pair, no rerun on another slice.
**Runtime cost: none** (svh multipliers). Quantizer cost: one decode + one Gram product per writer (~minutes a level).

## #106-01 · o_proj head act order on the real 0.8B quantizer (4070)

**Why.** w64 item 5 (order-fixed proxy): o_proj's 128-block order through a head permutation gains ~3%
(0.971 / 0.970 / 0.971 at K2 / 3 / 4) when the heaviest heads are stored **last** (exllamav3 quantizes the last stored
block first); stored first costs +10%. The proxy has misled on order before; check on ourq / exllamav3.

**Permutation** (`perm06.py`, per attention layer 3, 7, …, 23 of the 0.8B: 8 q heads × 256, 2 KV heads, groups of
4 q heads): head trace = the trace of the calib H_in (o_proj.input) diagonal over the head's 256 columns. Stored order
= w64's `rev`: KV groups by ascending total trace, heads inside a group by ascending trace (heaviest last). Folded
into the checkpoint: q_proj rows (per head 512 = q 256 + gate 256, HF's interleave), k_proj / v_proj rows (per KV
head 256), o_proj columns; q_norm / k_norm are per head_dim (shared): unchanged. The o_proj H_in is permuted to match
(a private Hessian dir: links to every other group).
**bf16 gate (before quantizing).** The permuted checkpoint vs the original on the same tokens (`bf16chk.py`, 8
sequences × 1,024 tokens from g08-fit1 docs, eager bf16): logits bit-identical → PASS. If not bit-identical: PASS only
if max |Δlogit| is no larger than the float-reassociation scale (the same model run with o_proj's input order
permuted and un-permuted in f32 on the same inputs) and mean KL(original ‖ permuted) ≤ 1e-6 with top-1 agreement
≥ 99.99%; the numbers are reported either way. Fail → stop, diagnose.
**Amendment 1 (2026-11-07 ~6:35 AM CST, after the gate's data; the rule above is not edited).** The gate as written
**fails**: A vs B is not bit-identical, max |Δlogit| 4.125 (= the reassociation scale C − A, 4.125), but mean
KL(A ‖ B) **7.2e-3** and top-1 agreement **98.0%** (8 × 1,024 tokens of g08-fit1). Diagnosis (`/data/yb/w75/diag/`):
- **B == C bit for bit**: the permuted checkpoint computes exactly the original function with only o_proj's summation
  order changed; the q / k / v / o fold itself is exact.
- The original is deterministic run to run (bitwise). On the same input, the permuted-order o_proj differs from the
  natural one in 0.1–0.2% of its bf16 outputs (layers whose KV-group order is kept) to 38–43% (group order swapped:
  L7 / 11 / 23), and **both orders are equally accurate** vs f64 (rel 1.9–2.3e-3 each; permuted ≤ natural on every
  layer and sequence). So the difference is bf16 output rounding of one GEMM, which the 0.8B's bf16 forward amplifies
  into KL(A ‖ B) mean 7.2e-3 with a heavy tail (median 4.4e-4, p90 2.6e-3, p99 0.17, max 1.2; the same with
  torch's bf16 reduced-precision reduction off: 7.1e-3). The rule's 1e-6 / 99.99% bars assumed reassociation noise
  would be negligible; it is not, on this model.
- Decision: the gate is read as **PASS on the stronger criterion B == C bitwise** (exactness of the fold), and the arms
  are scored as designed, with the perm arm's o_proj **un-permuted to the natural order**, so this reassociation noise
  does not enter the #106-01 comparison. The plumbing check below is reported against this noise, not against 1e-4.
- Finding for the lead (beyond #106): on the 0.8B (HF bf16 path) a one-GEMM summation-order change alone moves the
  logits by KL ~7e-3 (≈ 8% of K3's KLD), ~50× the "~1.5e-4 KL of kernel noise" in `proxy2b/common.py`. Any 0.8B arm
  that changes the evaluation arithmetic (not only the weights) carries noise of this size.

**Arms** (K ∈ {3, 2}, seeds 0–2; the pl stack of w64: edy + swj siblings, plain edy writers, α = 0 = `mix-p{K}{x}`):
- **natural:** `mix-p{K}{x}` as is (its o_proj = d1112's `q-k{K}ste{x}` shards, decoded).
- **perm:** the same 150 weights with the 6 o_proj requantized from the permuted checkpoint with the level's own args
  (`q2b.sh 08 K i8`, c18 H_out, σ_out 0.01, seed offset s, `--only` the o_proj), decoded as shipped (`deq.py`) and
  un-permuted to the natural column order: exactly the permuted model served with q / k / v rows permuted, with q / k /
  v / everything else held fixed so that only o_proj's codes differ.
- **No-op gate:** the same `--only` run on the original checkpoint must reproduce d1112's o_proj tensors bit for bit;
  else that (K, s)'s control becomes mix-p with the no-op o_proj (drift is not read as the effect).
- **Plumbing check (seed 0, K3, reported):** the perm arm scored on the permuted checkpoint with all of q / k / v / o in
  the stored order must give the same KLD as the un-permuted arm (≤ 1e-4 relative).
**Statistics.** (a) quantizer level: rel = tr(E H Eᵀ) / tr(W H Wᵀ) of each o_proj (decoded, raw calib H, natural order),
ratio perm / natural; geo over 6 layers × 3 seeds per K. (b) model level: KLD perm / natural on g08-search1 (deciding),
paired (pcmp.py), geo over seeds per K; g08-verify1 checked.
**Rule:**
- **PASS → propose a 27B pair (not run here):** (a) geo ≤ **0.985** at both K and every seed's 6-layer geo < 1, and
  (b) search geo < 1.000 at both K.
- **FAIL:** (a) geo > 0.995 at both K, or (b) search geo > 1.005 at either K.
- **PARTIAL:** anything else (e.g. real quantizer gain but no model-level direction): reported; the lead decides.
- Reported: per-layer rel ratios, head traces and the chosen orders, verify ratios, pooled z.
**Runtime cost: none** (a permutation folded into the stored tensors).
