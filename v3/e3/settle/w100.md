## #128 · Sub-1 hygiene (item 4)

Job `sub1`: per tensor × sample, i8 K0.125 / 0.5 / 1 at today's settings with the production H_out (bn1920 h_F,
σ_out 0.01): arm **cur**. Plus arm **ext** (coarse grid 0.1..3.9 instead of 0.1..1.9, quantized only if it picks
another scale) and arm **drift** (scale search with this tensor's measured drift d_all instead of 1.08, quantized
only if the scale moves).

1. **C9 at sub-1 (A-3, R15).** Per row n: e_n = d_n M d_nᵀ / w_n M w_nᵀ with M = H_pub (C9's metric), d after the
   per-output-channel refit; clip [0, 0.5]; c_n = (1 − e_n)^(−α/2) with v3.toml's α (gdn_out = ssm_out 3, attn_o −1;
   down 0 → e statistics only). Cell = (K, family); c_mean = mean over the cell's rows (3 tensors × 2 samples × 128).
   **Fires if c_mean > 1.3 at any K < 1.5** for gdn_out (and, the same rule mirrored for the negative α, if attn_o's
   c_mean < 1 / 1.3). If it fires: **propose α(K) = α · clip(K − 1, 0, 1)** (C9 off at K ≤ 1, half at K1.5, full
   from K2). Real-code cross-check: the w92 writers at K0.125 / 0.875 (reported, G3-gated).
2. **g_scale edge (A-6, R23).** exllamav3's search picks g from a coarse grid 0.1..1.9 plus a fine step; its weight
   input is tiles × g, so the reconstruction's amplitude is ~1/g and R23's "0.5 floor" is this grid's **top edge**
   (g = 2.05 = 1.9 + 2 × 0.075 with no parabola). Edge = coarse index at the end and fine index at the outer end.
   Histogram per K over the 27 tensors × 2 samples (the proxy's tile sample = exllamav3's over the whole tensor) and
   w92's real logs. **Fires if > 10% of a K's tensor × samples sit at an edge** → propose extending the grid on
   that side by ×2 (top: coarse to 3.9, the reciprocal of R23's "0.25"). Reported with it: the ext arm's held-out
   two-sided rel_B vs cur (geo, per K and group), so the lead sees what the extension buys.
3. **Drift below K1 (A-7).** d_all = RMS of every tile the trellis received / RMS of the pre-feedback rows (what the
   scale search's × drift emulates); also the fork comment's definition (mean block RMS over the recursion / the
   first block's), reported. Per K: D(K) = geo mean of d_all over the 54 (cur arm). **Fires if |D(1)/1.08 − 1| ≥ 0.5%,
   or D(K) ≥ 1.2 or ≤ 1.0 at K0.5 / K0.125** (A-7: "≥ 0.5% at K ≥ 1, ≥ 1.2 at K0.5"; ≤ 1.0 added: the clamp then
   overstates the drift by ≥ 8%). If it fires: propose the
   measured table for that K; the drift arm's held-out geo rel_B ratio vs cur is reported as its size (proposal only if
   ≤ 1.000).
4. **Zero-tensor floor (D-2, R22).** rel after refit vs zero (Q = 0 gives rel 1) at K0.125: (a) **real codes**: the 13

5. **suh / svh underflow (R14).** `w100/scan100.py`, read-only, niced, on gpubox: every level / job directory with

## #129 · H_out shape: σ_out × tempering h^p, held out (item 8)

**H_out halves** (G2): the bn1920 collection's 15 blocks of 128 sequences, block-normalized as production, averaged
over **E** = even blocks 0, 2, …, 14 (the quantizer's), **O1** = odd blocks {1, 5, 9, 13} (pick), **O2** = {3, 7, 11}
(judge); also O = all odd and F = all 15 (= production bn1920).

Job `hshape`: per tensor × sample, i8 **K0.5 / 2.5 / 4**, today's σ_in and scale, the quantizer's H_out =
diag(h_E^p), normalized by its whole-tensor mean as prepare_H_out, + σ_out I. Arms: σ_out ∈ {0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1}
× p ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1} (12; **current = σ_out 0.01, p 1**), plus "full" (h_F, 0.01, 1 = production; reported: what the
half collection costs). Every Q is scored under H_X (A, B1, B2) × h (E, O1, O2, O, F) and one-sided.

**Rule (#114's arithmetic, two independent held-out halves on both sides):** r(t, s, K, arm) = rel(arm) /
rel(current), two-sided, paired per tensor × sample.
1. **Pick** on X1 = (H_B1, h_O1): arm* = the lowest geo mean of r_X1 over the cell's tensors × 2 samples.
2. **Judge** on X2 = (H_B2, h_O2): gain g = 1 − geo mean of r_X2(arm*). A tensor agrees if its geo mean of
   r_X2(arm*) over the 2 samples is < 1.
3. A change is **proposed** for the cell only if arm* ≠ current, **g ≥ 2 %** and **≥ ⅔ of the cell's tensors agree**
   (all 27 ≥ 18; inputs group 15 ≥ 10; rest 12 ≥ 8; family 3 ≥ 2). Otherwise "σ_out 0.01, p 1 stands" for the cell.
   Cells: 3 K × (all + 2 groups + 9 families) = 36. A global setting (v3.toml `hout.sigma_reg_out`, a new exponent)
   is proposed only from the "all" cells; group / family passes are reported as candidate overrides.
4. arm* on a grid edge (σ_out 0.003 or 0.1, p 0.5) is flagged: a proposal there needs the grid extended (not done
   without the lead).
5. Reported, not deciding: per-cell surfaces on X = (B, O) and in-sample (A, E); pick-on-X2 / judge-on-X1 (swap);
   one-sided error of the same quantizations; "full" vs current (half vs whole collection).

Caveat: the quantizer's h is half the collection (8 of 15 blocks), so the held-out optimum may sit at more smoothing
(lower p / higher σ_out) than production's full collection would want; a pass is a direction for a 27B pair, not a
setting by itself (ranked.md: "a 27B pair only if it passes").

Runtime cost: none (quantizer setting).
