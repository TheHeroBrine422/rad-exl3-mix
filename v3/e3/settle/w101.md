## #130 Output-row tile interleave (B-1)

**Interleave P16** (composable `perm2.py` `gperm(R, 16)`, the arm the composable model picked, A4): the R output rows
are cut into R/16 tiles of 16 rows (one EXL3 output tile each); stored 128-row block b holds tiles b, b + nb, b + 2nb,
… (nb = R/128 blocks, 8 tiles per block), i.e. every stored Hadamard block holds 16-row tiles of 8 different original
128-row blocks (= 8 different heads for qkv / z; for q_proj, q-half and gate-half tiles of 2 heads). h and the row's
weights move with the row; H_in, seeds, settings do not change. The permutation does not depend on h.

### #130-01 (a): realized f2 on the real quantizer (card 0)

- **Tensors:** L10 `linear_attn.in_proj_qkv`, L10 `linear_attn.in_proj_z`, L11 `self_attn.q_proj`, L39
  `self_attn.q_proj`. **Levels:** K2.5 and K4, i8 (the config's codebook). **Seeds:** seed offset 0 and 1.
- **Arms:** N = natural rows (the real checkpoint and bn1920 H_out); P = the same tensor with W rows and h permuted

  the originals after un-permuting). Both arms: `python -m rxq quant --keys ... --save-wq` with `v3.toml` as of the
  rule commit (H_out blocks path, #117 block order, tile CD on attn_qg, σ as configured; C9 / swj do not touch
  readers), card 0 under card0.lock + ram.lock (shared). f2 of P is computed after un-permuting Wq to natural order,
  against natural W and h (it equals f2 in the permuted basis; the script checks both to 1e-9).
- **Statistic:** r(t, K, s) = f2_P / f2_N. Per tensor: R_t = geo mean over K ∈ {2.5, 4} × s ∈ {0, 1}.
- **Pass (gate for (c)):** **R(L10 qkv) ≤ 0.90.** Scope of (c), decided from (a) before (c) runs: qkv always;
  q_proj (attn_qg) added if the geo mean of R over L11 / L39 q_proj ≤ 0.95; z added if R(L10 z) ≤ 0.95.
- **Reported, not deciding:** each r; the composable model's predicted ratio (`perm2.py` g16 / nat) per tensor and the
  realized share ln r / ln r_model; f1 (unweighted) ratios; the proxy_err logged by rxq; quant time per arm.

### #130-02 (b): out-of-sample stability of the head-level h structure (CPU, colo)

- **Data:** the 15 blocks of 128 sequences of the 27B Σdy² collection (gpubox `/data/yaqa/hdiag/hdiag-snapN-dy`,
  cumulative means; differenced as `mkhalf.py` does), each block normalised to the same per-tensor total (bn), for
  every in_proj_qkv (48), in_proj_z (48) and q_proj (16). h_E = mean over the even blocks 0, 2, …, 14 (8), h_O = mean
  over the odd blocks (7). **Gate G-b:** the mean over all 15 blocks / its mean equals hout-bn / its mean to 1e-5 on
  every key (else nothing is read).
- **Model** (composable `perm.py` / `perm2.py`, high-rate, unchanged): v = h · r² (r² = row mean square of the bf16
  W); per stored 128-row block, M = S diag(v_block / mean(v)) Sᵀ with S the signed 128-Hadamard of `perm2.py` (rng 1);
  the quantizer's factor from M_A + 0.01 I (σ_out 0.01), unit block-LDL in perm.py's order (Cholesky of the reversed
  matrix). Cost of factor A scored in metric B: c(A→B) = Σ_blocks tr(L_A⁻¹ M_B L_A⁻ᵀ) (the high-rate two-sided error
  with the factor from A, measured in the undamped metric B); c(A→A) is the in-sample value.
- **Arms:** N (natural) and P16 (h-independent). Reported also (not deciding): **B16**, a balanced 16-row assignment
  fitted on A (tiles sorted by mean v_A, dealt in snake order over the blocks, composable A5), scored the same way.
- **Gain:** G_in = 1 − c_P(A→A) / c_N(A→A), G_out = 1 − c_P(A→B) / c_N(A→B), per tensor; per family the geo mean of
  the cost ratios over its tensors; both directions (A, B) = (E, O) and (O, E), averaged (geo mean of the ratios).
  kept = G_out / G_in.
- **Pass:** **kept ≥ 0.75 for in_proj_qkv** (the family (c) depends on). q_proj / z: the same rule decides whether
  they may join (c) (with (a)'s scope rule). Reported: per-tensor kept, the per-head (per natural 128-row block)
  mean-h Spearman ρ between halves, the full-set (bn1920 → bn1920) model ratios for reference. Halves hold ~half the
  sequences of the production estimate, so the out-of-sample loss measured here overstates the production one; noted,
  not corrected.

## #131 Per-section K inside fused q_proj (q | gate) and in_proj_qkv (q | k | v) (B-2)

**Runtime assumption (lead):** the tensor stays fused and uses per-128-row-block K (the slab head's kernel feature,
#34-02), so ~0 runtime cost. **Section boundaries:** in_proj_qkv rows are [q 2048 | k 2048 | v 6144] (HF
`modeling_qwen3_5.py`: `torch.split(mixed_qkv, [key_dim, key_dim, value_dim])`), boundaries at rows 2048 and 4096 =
128 × 16, 128 × 32. q_proj rows are per head [q 256 | gate 256] (`q_proj(x).view(..., -1, 2·head_dim)` then
`chunk(2)`), 24 heads: every 128-row block is all-q or all-gate (blocks 4j, 4j+1 q; 4j+2, 4j+3 gate). The script
asserts both.

- **Proxy:** w87's two-sided block proxy (`w87/settle87.py`: P2 transforms of one aligned 128-row output block,
  `ldlq2` = the fork's `ldlq_2hess` restricted to a 128-row block, which is exactly the blocks path, bn1920 H_out with
  σ_out 0.01 normalised over all N rows, block-LDL(16), per-output-channel refit, exllamav3 scale search with ourq's
  drift), here with the **published calib H_in** for quantizer and scoring (no held-out split: the question is rate
  allocation, not damping), σ = today's (0.005, input group, K ≥ 1), i8 at every K, natural (rev) LDLQ order.
  Not modelled (same for every arm): #117 block order, tile CD on attn_qg, the per-input-channel su refit (shared by
  the sections of a fused tensor), swj / C9 (not on readers).
- **Tensors (8):** attn_qg (q_proj) L3 / L19 / L39 / L63; ssm_qkv (in_proj_qkv) L2 / L10 / L30 / L50.
- **Blocks:** per section 8 distinct 128-row blocks drawn at random (numpy rng seed 131 + layer), split into halves
  A (first 4 drawn) and B (last 4). qkv: 24 blocks per tensor (q 8 of 16, k 8 of 16, v 8 of 48); q_proj: 16 (q 8 of
  48, gate 8 of 48).
- **Levels:** K ∈ {1, 1.25, …, 5.5} (19, quarter steps), i8. The g_scale search per (tensor, K) on the P2 tile sample of
  the whole tensor (as production: one search per tensor), used by every block of the tensor.
- **Per block b, K:** e_b(K) = two-sided error after the refit (w87's e2 under H), ref_b = Σ_i h_i w_i H w_iᵀ.
  ref_b is computed for **every** block of the tensor (no quantization needed).
- **Section error from half X:** E_s^X(K) = R_s · Σ_{b ∈ X ∩ s} e_b(K) / Σ_{b ∈ X ∩ s} ref_b (ratio estimator),
  R_s = Σ_{all b ∈ s} ref_b. One K: E_1^X(K) = Σ_s E_s^X(K).
- **Equal bytes:** Σ_s n_s K_s = n B (rows n_s; same in-features), on the quarter grid within [1, 5.5]: qkv K_q + K_k
  + 3 K_v = 5B; q_proj K_q + K_g = 2B. Budgets B ∈ {2.5, 4}.
- **Held-out choice:** K* = argmin over feasible splits of Σ_s E_s^A(K_s); scored on B: E^B(K*). Bit-equivalent gain
  g_B = K_eq − B, where E_1^B(K_eq) = E^B(K*) (log-linear interpolation of E_1^B between grid points; one K at B is
  feasible, so the in-sample gain is ≥ 0; held out it can be < 0). Symmetric (choose on B, score on A): g_A. Cell gain
  g = (g_A + g_B) / 2. Cells: 8 tensors × 2 budgets = 16.
- **Pass:** **mean g over the 16 cells ≥ 0.10 bit.** A split that lands on a grid edge (1 or 5.5) is flagged.
- **Reported, not deciding:** g per family and budget, the in-sample gains, the chosen splits, the section energy
  shares R_s / Σ R, the high-rate section ceiling ½ Σ_s (n_s/n) log2(ρ̄ / ρ_s) with ρ_s = R_s / n_s (codes D-2's
  quantity, Gaussian slopes), and the gain in KL-proxy terms (1 − E^B(K*) / E_1^B(B)).
- **27B pair:** only if it passes; its design (fixed codes from two existing levels, rows of one level per section,
  bf16 dequant splice) is fixed in an amendment before its data.
