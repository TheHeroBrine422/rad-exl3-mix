## Arms (fixed codes; only the writers' row multipliers differ)

C9 per output row n of a writer: e_n = (q_n − w_n) M (q_n − w_n)ᵀ / w_n M w_nᵀ (q = the job's decoded weight, w = bf16,
M = the group's raw h08 H_in), c_n = (1 − clip(e_n, 0, e_clip))^(−α/2), folded into the row. Computed with the shipped
`rxq.post.c9_tensor` (same decode as the jobs), only α and e_clip vary. Writers: attn_o (o_proj), gdn_out (out_proj),
down (α 0 in every arm: unchanged). swj touches gate / up / down only, so the arms commute with it.
- **off**: c = 1.
- **cur** (C9 as today): α attn_o −1, gdn_out 3, e_clip 0.5.
- **ak**: α(K) = α · clip(K − 1, 0, 1), e_clip 0.5. Identical to **off** at K0.5 / K1 and to **cur** at K2 (by
  construction; those cells are not re-scored, the identical file is reused and the ratio is exactly 1 vs it).
- **e3**: α as today, e_clip 0.3. Where no row of any writer has e > 0.3 it equals **cur** (then not re-scored).

**Gate G1 (before any score is read):** for every writer of every quant, the c of **cur** recomputed by my script equals
the job's own C9 sidecar (`c9-<key>.safetensors`) to ≤ 1e-6 relative. Fail → stop and report.
**Gate G2:** the rebuilt g08-search1 has 196,542 rows and the same (orig, tokens, score_from) per document as the old
kld.json; same for g08-fit1. Fail → stop.

## Scoring and statistics

`proxy2b/evalq.py` on g08-search1 (gen-masked mean KL vs bf16). Pairs with `proxy2b/pcmp.py` (per-document bootstrap,
2,000 resamples): ratio r = KL(arm) / KL(cur) per (K, seed), its 95% per-document bootstrap interval, z. Also off / cur.

**Pass rule (task):** an arm (off, ak, e3) passes if **r ≤ 0.99 at K0.5 and at K1 on both seeds**, and at **K2 it is not
worse than cur by more than 0.3%: r ≤ 1.003 on both seeds** (stricter reading; the geo over seeds is reported too).
K1.5: reported, not deciding. Several arms pass → the one with the lowest geo r over (K0.5, K1) × seeds is proposed,
noting ties within the paired SE (~1%). No arm passes → keep C9 as today (report the off / cur ratios per K).
**Reported:** c statistics per K and family (mean / max / share of rows at the clip; e mean / max) per arm, pooled over
layers, per seed; KL and top-1 of every arm; off / cur per K (does C9 pay at all at low K?).
**Runtime cost: none** (C9 folds into svh; quantizer / scales only).
