  ds8 for packing; K2.5 / K4 with swj scales 6c57a3d1, K6 without).
- **Packs:** both arms packed from their `final.recipe` with hostd's pack_cmd (rad10 home-pack, `rad-convert --no-error`).
  Arm M through a **shim** until #177-02's importer reads `codebook=mad`: a hard-link copy of `final/` whose `stamp.json`

  max-num-seqs 1. Arm I with home `w139s3/home-base3` (rad10-perf's exl3i), arm M with `w139s3/home-hq` (EXL3I_BENCH_IQRH
  1: the hash decode in mul1i8's slot, A 0xd0474b / B 0x2545F491, values = exl3hash's f16 q · step). **Sanity:** arm M's
  KLD within ×1.5 of arm I's and top-1 within 2 points at every level, else a decode mismatch (the bench path was only
  exercised at K2.625 / K4.125 in stage 3): that level is reported as "not measured" and the lead decides; it is not a
  quality result.

## Statistic and readout

- Per level and part (x, c): R = mean KLD(M) / mean KLD(I) over the part's positions, paired per-document bootstrap 95 %
  CI (2,000 resamples). Pooled: the two parts' ratios combined with equal weight (geometric mean, as the search's per-part
  accept), CI from bootstrapping documents within each part. Also reported: top-1 agreement, KLD, Δbpw-equivalent
  (ln R / the i8 ladder's local slope from the three twins' scores).
- **Expectation (#177 stage 3 + head bench):** R ≈ 1 (stage 3: ×1.001 / ×1.037 calib, pooled with xcorp ×1.006 / ×1.011).
- **Readout labels per level:** "≈" if the pooled CI contains 1.00 or the pooled R ≤ 1.03; "costs" if the CI lies above 1.00
  and R > 1.03; "better" if it lies below 1.00.
- **Stop rule ("off", lead note):** any level with the pooled R > 1.08, or the pooled CI entirely above 1.05, or a failed
  confounder / sanity check → stop before stage C and report; else continue to stage C.
- **Proxy MSE vs the twins (no slice):** per level, geometric mean over the trunk tensors of the jobs' logged proxy error
  ratio M / I (level `proxy.jsonl`), and per family. Expected ≈ 1 (stage 2's real-tensor proxy ×1.008–1.010 at L16).
  "Off" if a level's geo ratio > 1.03 (then the KLD is read with that in mind, and the lead is told).
